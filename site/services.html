Fractional & Virtual CISO

Is a fractional CISO right for you?

Most small and mid-sized businesses need CISO-level judgment long before they can justify a full-time, six-figure security executive. A fractional or virtual CISO gives you that leadership on a schedule and budget that fits.

Signs it's time

  • A customer, prime contractor, or insurer sent a security questionnaire you can't confidently answer
  • An audit or assessment is coming: CMMC, HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI DSS, or the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)
  • Your board or investors are asking who owns cyber risk
  • Your IT team or MSP is stretched thin and security has no single owner
  • Employees are already using AI tools and no one has set the rules
  • You've had an incident, a near miss, or a failed phishing test

Our Foundation

Servant leadership, with people at the center of security.

Security tools and tactics are necessary, but they are never enough. Every serious breach we study has a human moment: a rushed approval, an unasked question, an alert nobody owned. That is why we lead by serving your people first, and why we keep a trained, accountable human in the loop for every decision that matters, including decisions made with the help of AI.

01

We serve, then lead

We listen to your team before we recommend anything, explain the “why” in plain English, and measure our success by how capable and confident your people become, not by how dependent they are on us.

02

Humans in the loop

Technology finds and flags; people decide. We design processes so a named person reviews and owns high-impact actions, from approving payments to acting on AI-generated advice.

03

Culture over checklists

Policies only work when people believe in them. We build security habits through coaching, tabletop exercises, and recognition, so staff report mistakes early instead of hiding them.

04

Quality work, honestly delivered

We tell you plainly what matters, what does not, and when you do not need us. Every engagement ends with clear ownership, documentation your team can use, and skills that stay with you.
