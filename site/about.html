About Nexus Security Advisors.

A veteran-owned cybersecurity leadership firm helping small and mid-sized businesses align security, compliance, and artificial intelligence (AI) with business outcomes.

Founder & Principal vCISO

Dan Sorensen

Dan is a fractional virtual chief information security officer (vCISO) and AI governance advisor who translates cyber threats into boardroom decisions. A U.S. Air Force and Air National Guard veteran with 22+ years of service, he builds security programs that meet major U.S. and international security, healthcare, defense, and AI standards, including those from the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) and the International Organization for Standardization (ISO). He leads as a servant leader: he mentors veterans and newcomers starting cybersecurity careers, volunteers in the security community, and brings the work ethic and commitment to quality he learned from his family to every engagement. He speaks English, Spanish, Portuguese, and Italian.

He contributes to the Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP) AI Exchange, has 77 published contributions to Forbes and Fast Company, co-authored Advanced Cyber Threat Intelligence and Hunting and The AI Mindset, and is a PhD candidate in Cybersecurity Leadership at Capitol Technology University.
