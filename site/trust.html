Privacy notice

What we collect and why

Last updated November 18, 2026

Information you choose to send us

When you email us or send a request through our Get Started page, we receive what you provide, such as your name, work email, company, and details about your security needs. We use it only to respond, prepare for and deliver consulting services, and keep records required for business purposes.

Website analytics

We use PostHog, a product analytics service hosted in the United States, to understand which pages are useful and to improve the site. It records page views and general interactions such as clicks, along with technical data like browser type and approximate location. Your IP address is used only to work out that approximate location and is then discarded, not stored. It may also make a session recording of how pages are used, such as scrolling, clicks, and page changes, with everything typed into forms hidden. Outside Europe, it sets a first-party cookie and a local storage identifier. Analytics requests pass through our own domain to PostHog. We configure it so that what you type into the intake form, checklists, and the password checker is excluded, and we honor your browser's Do Not Track setting. If you visit from the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, PostHog runs in cookieless mode: it stores nothing on your device, and visits are counted with a one-way code that PostHog's servers build from your IP address, browser details and a value that changes every day, so your visits on different days are not linked. We rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used. You can turn analytics off at any time under Privacy choices below, and we honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. We do not use analytics data for advertising or sell it. You can block analytics with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker without affecting how the site works.

To understand which channels bring us clients, this site also remembers in your browser’s local storage, for up to 90 days (outside Europe only), how you first arrived (for example a search engine, an AI assistant, LinkedIn, or a campaign link) and the page you landed on. It contains no identifier and is never sent anywhere unless you submit the intake form, in which case it is included with your request. When you submit the intake form, and analytics is running for you, we also include PostHog’s random browser identifier so we can see which pages led to your request. It is linked to your request only in our CRM and is not sent if you turned analytics off or visit from Europe. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

We also use HubSpot, our marketing platform, operated under HubSpot's privacy policy. Outside Europe, its tracking code sets cookies that recognize a returning browser and record which pages it visits, so we can see how people find and use the site. We don't load it for visitors from Europe. It does not collect what you type into the intake form. You can block these cookies with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker without affecting how the site works, or turn analytics off below, which also stops HubSpot's tracking code.

Privacy choices

There is no cookie banner because visitors from Europe get cookieless analytics and HubSpot isn't loaded for them. Anyone can turn analytics off for this browser; this saves only that one choice in your browser's local storage. Turning it off stops PostHog and HubSpot and clears what this site stored for analytics and lead-source attribution.

Tools that run in your browser

The interactive checklists and the intake save your progress in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot see this data. You can clear it at any time with the “Reset” or “Clear my answers” buttons, or by clearing your browser's site data. Avoid entering sensitive details on shared computers.

Consultation requests

When you choose “Send my request” on our Get Started page, your answers are sent over an encrypted connection to our client management system, Zoho customer relationship management (CRM), operated by Zoho under its privacy policy. We use them only to respond to you, schedule a call, and prepare for our conversation, and we keep them only as long as needed for that purpose and our business records. We do not publish a public booking calendar; after reviewing your request, we send you a private scheduling link.

Who we are and our legal basis

Nexus Security Advisors is responsible for the information described here; contact us at info@nexussecurityadvisors.com. We process the information you send so we can respond to your request and take steps you ask for before any engagement, based on your consent and our legitimate interest in answering business inquiries. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.

Where your information is stored and for how long

Requests are stored in Zoho CRM in Zoho's United States data center and copied to our HubSpot account so our marketing and sales records stay in one place. If you contact us from outside the United States, your information is transferred to the United States under Zoho's and HubSpot's data-transfer safeguards. We keep inquiry records only as long as needed to respond, provide services, and meet business and legal record-keeping needs, and we delete them on request where the law allows.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information.
  • We don't use your information for advertising.
  • We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct, or delete information you've sent us by emailing info@nexussecurityadvisors.com. Depending on where you live, laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) may give you additional rights, including the right to object, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We will honor applicable requests and will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Confidentiality of client information

Client engagement information is treated as confidential and protected with access controls, encryption, and least privilege. We are happy to sign a mutual nondisclosure agreement (NDA) before detailed discussions.

Vulnerability disclosure policy

Report a security issue

We welcome reports from security researchers and follow coordinated disclosure practices aligned with International Organization for Standardization and International Electrotechnical Commission (ISO/IEC) 29147.

How to report

Email security@nexussecurityadvisors.com with the subject “Security report.” Please include the affected URL or system, steps to reproduce, potential impact, and how to contact you.

Scope

nexussecurityadvisors.com and its subdomains, and our email domain. Third-party services we use (such as our hosting and scheduling providers) should be reported to those providers directly.

Please do

  • Act in good faith and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure.
  • Interact only with your own data and use the minimum access needed to demonstrate the issue.
  • Stop and report as soon as you encounter any personal or client data.

Please don't

  • Run denial-of-service, spam, or automated high-volume scanning.
  • Use social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks.
  • Access, modify, or keep data that isn't yours.

Our commitment

We aim to acknowledge reports within three business days, keep you informed as we investigate, and credit you publicly if you'd like.

Safe harbor

If you make a good-faith effort to follow this policy, we consider your research authorized, we will not pursue or support legal action against you (including under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act or anti-circumvention laws such as the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)), and if a third party takes legal action over research that followed this policy, we will make it known that you acted in compliance with it. If you're unsure whether something is in scope, ask us first.

Email security

Verifying it's really us

Our domain publishes Sender Policy Framework (SPF), DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM), and a Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance (DMARC) policy so receiving mail servers can reject messages that spoof us, plus Transport Layer Security (TLS) reporting to monitor encrypted delivery. Legitimate email from us comes only from addresses ending in @nexussecurityadvisors.com. We will never ask you to pay an invoice to new bank details by email alone. If something looks off, contact us at info@nexussecurityadvisors.com using an address you already know, not by replying to the suspicious message.

Accessibility

Built for everyone

We design this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, including keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient color contrast, and support for reduced-motion settings. Every page has an Accessibility & language button (bottom left) that can read the page aloud using your browser’s built-in voice and open a free machine translation in Spanish, Italian or Portuguese.
