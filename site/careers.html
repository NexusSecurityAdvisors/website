Start Your Cyber Career

There is room for you in cybersecurity.

Cybersecurity needs people from every background: students, veterans, career changers, and anyone curious about how things work and how to protect them. You do not need a computer science degree to start. This page lays out free paths, free and low-cost certifications, and ways to find a mentor, plus how to volunteer with Nexus.

Explore Roles

Entry points into cybersecurity.

Explore salaries, demand, and career paths on CyberSeek, and see how roles are defined in the NICE Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity.

Security analyst

Watch for and respond to alerts in a security operations center. Great first role for problem solvers.

Governance, risk & compliance

Turn laws and standards into policies and evidence. Ideal for organized communicators, including many veterans.

Digital forensics & incident response

Investigate what happened after an attack and help organizations recover.

Penetration tester

Test systems, with permission, to find weaknesses before attackers do.

Cloud security

Secure Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud environments.

AI security & governance

Help organizations use artificial intelligence (AI) safely. A fast-growing field.

Your First 90 Days

A simple, free plan to get started.

Days 1–30: Foundations

  • Free course: Harvard CS50 Introduction to Cybersecurity
  • Learn networking basics at Cisco Networking Academy
  • Try beginner rooms on TryHackMe

Days 31–60: First credential

  • Study for the free-training ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)
  • Practice weekly on picoCTF challenges
  • Take our Nexus quizzes and monthly challenge

Days 61–90: Show your work

  • Build a home lab and write about it on GitHub or LinkedIn
  • Join a local Information Systems Security Association (ISSA) or Security BSides chapter
  • Ask for a mentor (below) and apply for internships or volunteer roles
