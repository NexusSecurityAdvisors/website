FAQ

Common questions

What is a fractional CISO (vCISO)?

A fractional or virtual CISO is a senior security executive who leads your security program part-time. You get CISO-level strategy, risk management, and board reporting without the cost of a full-time hire.

Who is a good fit for Nexus Security Advisors?

Small and mid-sized businesses in any industry, including financial services, healthcare, defense suppliers, manufacturing, technology, and professional services, that need executive security leadership, are facing an audit or assessment, or are rolling out AI and need it governed.

Can you help us prepare for CMMC?

Yes. We assess your current state against NIST SP 800-171 and CMMC requirements, identify gaps, and build a prioritized, owner-assigned plan to close them.

How do you approach AI governance?

We start by finding the AI tools and agents actually in use, then build AI risk into each initiative's business case, apply least privilege to AI agents, and align to frameworks such as NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act, and the OWASP AI Exchange.

Can we sign an NDA before sharing details?

Yes. We are happy to sign a mutual nondisclosure agreement (NDA) before any detailed discussion of your environment.

Do you replace our IT team or MSP?

No. We provide security leadership and oversight and work alongside your IT team or managed service provider, helping them prioritize and measure what matters.

How is a fractional CISO engagement priced?

Usually as a predictable monthly retainer sized to your needs, or a fixed fee for project work such as an assessment or readiness sprint. We recommend an option after the free discovery call.
