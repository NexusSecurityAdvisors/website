Your first session

What to expect

1. We reply within one business dayWe review your request and send you a private link to schedule a free 30-minute discovery call.
2. Discovery callWe ask a few follow-up questions and tell you plainly whether we're a fit.
3. Scoped proposalA short proposal with the recommended engagement, deliverables, and timeline.
4. KickoffWe confirm stakeholders, gather documents, and schedule the first working sessions.
5. First resultsAn early read on your top risks and quick wins, then a prioritized roadmap.
What should I have ready for the first call?

Nothing is required. If handy: any recent security questionnaire, audit or assessment results, your cyber insurance application, a list of key systems and vendors (including your MSP), and existing security policies.

Who from our side should join?

Ideally a business decision-maker (owner, CEO, COO, or CFO) and whoever runs IT today, whether internal staff or your MSP.

Can we sign an NDA before sharing details?

Yes. Just ask, and we'll put one in place before any detailed discussion.

Do you replace our IT team or MSP?

No. We provide security leadership and oversight and work alongside your IT team or MSP, helping them prioritize and measure what matters.

How is a fractional CISO engagement priced?

Usually a predictable monthly retainer sized to your needs, or a fixed fee for project work like an assessment or readiness sprint. We'll recommend an option after the discovery call.

Do you work with companies outside the U.S.?

We serve clients across the United States and can support organizations selling into the EU on requirements like the Cyber Resilience Act and the AI Act.
