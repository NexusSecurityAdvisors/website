AI consulting questions and answers

How much does AI consulting cost for a small business?

Published 2026 U.S. benchmarks put small and mid-sized business (SMB) AI readiness assessments at roughly $2,000 to $25,000[1][2], AI governance readiness assessments at $8,000 to $12,000[3], and AI advisory retainers at $1,500 to $8,000 per month[2][4]. Nexus packages start at $2,500 for an AI Readiness & Use-Case Sprint, $5,000 for an AI Governance Program, $1,500 per month for a Fractional AI Advisor, and $995 for a Leadership AI Workshop. See all pricing sources.

Why are your prices lower than large consulting firms?

We work only with small and mid-sized businesses, use fixed scopes instead of open-ended hourly billing, and the senior advisor you meet is the one who does the work. You pay for outcomes sized to your business, not for a large firm's overhead. Compare our starting prices with the published market ranges.

What happens during an AI Readiness & Use-Case Sprint?

Over two to three weeks we inventory the AI tools already in use (including unapproved “shadow AI”), interview leaders about goals, score your top use cases for value, effort, and risk, and deliver a prioritized 90-day roadmap with quick wins, required guardrails, and estimated costs. Want a head start? Try the free AI governance checklist and the AI return on investment calculator below.

Do we need AI governance if employees only use ChatGPT or Copilot?

Yes. Most AI risk at small businesses comes from everyday chat tools: confidential data pasted into personal accounts, over-shared files surfacing in Copilot answers, and AI-generated content sent to customers without review. A short acceptable-use policy, an approved-tools list, and the right plan settings prevent most of it. The free OWASP LLM Cybersecurity and Governance Checklist is a good place to start.

Is it safe to put company data into ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or other AI tools?

It depends on the plan and configuration. Business and enterprise plans typically offer single sign-on, admin controls, and contractual commitments not to train on your data; free personal accounts usually do not. Regulated data such as protected health information (PHI), cardholder data, or controlled unclassified information (CUI) needs approved tools and extra controls. See the safe-configuration checklist.

What is shadow AI, and how do you find it?

Shadow AI is any AI tool, browser extension, plug-in, or agent used without approval or security review. We find it through interviews, single sign-on and browser-extension reviews, network and cloud-access logs, and a quick employee survey, then bring the useful tools under governance instead of just banning them. Learn more in our safe-configuration checklist and the OWASP AI Exchange guidance on running an AI program.

Which AI regulations apply to a small business?

Existing privacy, consumer-protection, and sector rules (such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Safeguards Rule, and state privacy laws) already apply to AI. New AI-specific laws include Colorado's Senate Bill (SB) 26-189, effective January 1, 2027, the Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act (TRAIGA), and the EU AI Act if you serve customers in Europe. See the AI regulations summary and Regulatory Watch.

How much do business AI assistants such as Microsoft 365 Copilot cost?

Pricing changes often, so always check the vendor's page. As of October 31, 2026, Microsoft lists Microsoft 365 Copilot Business at $21 per user per month on an annual plan, with an $18 first-year promotional price through December 31, 2026 (Microsoft 365 Copilot pricing). Compare current business plans from other providers before you choose: ChatGPT Business and Enterprise (OpenAI), Claude Team and Enterprise (Anthropic), and Google Workspace with Gemini. Licenses are only part of the cost; see what AI really costs, or estimate your own payback with the AI ROI calculator.

How do you measure return on investment (ROI) from AI?

We set a baseline before rollout (hours spent, response times, error rates, or revenue per employee), pick two or three measurable use cases, and compare after 30, 60, and 90 days. Use cases that do not show results are paused rather than scaled. Try the AI ROI calculator below for a quick estimate, and see McKinsey's State of AI 2026 for why measurement matters: only about a third of organizations report enterprise-level profit impact from AI.

What is AI red teaming?

AI red teaming is authorized adversarial testing of an AI system: trying prompt injection, data-leakage, jailbreak, and agent-abuse attacks before real attackers do. Findings are mapped to the OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model (LLM) Applications, the OWASP AI Exchange, and MITRE ATLAS, with prioritized fixes. See the OWASP GenAI Red Teaming Guide and OWASP AI Testing Guide.

Can you help us build a custom AI chatbot or app?

Yes. We design, build, test, and support custom AI chatbots, assistants, and agents, hosted in your private cloud or on your own servers, so proprietary, personal, and health information stays under your control. Every build includes testing for accuracy, consistency, and made-up answers (hallucinations), plus security testing against the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications. See Custom & Private AI for deployment options, our build process, and a build vs. buy calculator.

How do you keep our AI program current after the first engagement?

Through the Fractional AI Advisor retainer: monthly usage and log reviews, quarterly re-testing after vendor model updates, policy and inventory updates, regulatory tracking, and an annual red-team and tabletop exercise. See who you need to maintain AI.

Do you work with businesses outside the United States?

Most clients are U.S.-based, and we also support organizations in the European Union and elsewhere, including EU AI Act readiness, through remote engagements. See AI policy by country for official guidance in other countries, including the European AI Office.

How can we tell if an email, call, video, or website visitor is an AI fake?

Use layered checks: provenance tools such as Content Credentials, reverse image search, call-back verification on known numbers, and bot detection on your website. Our free guide, How to Spot AI Fakes and AI Bots, walks through each method.
