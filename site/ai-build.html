Why Build Your Own

When your own AI makes sense, and when it does not.

Benefits of your own AI

  • Proprietary knowledge stays yours: pricing, designs, playbooks, and client files are not sent to a public chatbot.
  • Personal and health data protection: personally identifiable information (PII) and protected health information (PHI) stay in systems you control, on services covered by a business associate agreement (BAA) where required (Microsoft, Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud).
  • Answers from your own documents, with citations, so staff can check where an answer came from.
  • Permission-aware: employees only get answers from documents they are already allowed to see.
  • Fits your workflow: connect to your customer relationship management (CRM), ticketing, document, or scheduling systems.
  • Predictable control: you choose the model, the version, where data lives, and how long logs are kept.

When buying is smarter

  • Your needs are general (writing, summarizing, brainstorming) and involve no sensitive data.
  • Fewer users mean per-user licenses cost less than building and running your own.
  • You have no one to own the system after launch.
  • The tools you already pay for (for example Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace) can be configured to meet the need.

We will tell you plainly which path fits. Use the build vs. buy calculator below for a first look.

Testing & Validity

How we test AI for accuracy, consistency, and made-up answers.

AI models can “hallucinate,” producing confident answers that are wrong or invented. You cannot eliminate this completely, but you can measure it, reduce it, and catch it before it reaches a customer.

What we test

  • Accuracy: a “golden” set of real questions with approved answers, scored before launch and after every change.
  • Groundedness: every answer must be supported by the cited source; unsupported claims are flagged.
  • Consistency: the same question asked many ways and many times should get the same substance.
  • Honest refusals: when the answer is not in your documents, the assistant says so instead of guessing.
  • Safety and security: prompt injection, data leakage, and attempts to see documents a user should not.
  • Regression: re-run the full test set whenever the vendor updates the model.

How we reduce made-up answers

  • Ground answers in your documents with citations (retrieval).
  • Instruct the model to answer only from provided sources.
  • Lower randomness settings for factual tasks.
  • Add automated groundedness checks before an answer is shown.
  • Keep a human reviewer on high-impact outputs.
  • Track a hallucination rate on a monthly quality report.

Methods and free tools we draw on: NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile, OWASP AI Testing Guide, Ragas, promptfoo, garak, and the OWASP GenAI Red Teaming Guide.
